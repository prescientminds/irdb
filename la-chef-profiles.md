## 1. Michael Cimarusti

**Current Restaurant(s):**
- **Providence** | 5955 Melrose Ave, Hollywood | Seafood-focused fine dining | 3 Michelin stars (2025)
- **Connie & Ted's** | West Hollywood | Casual New England-style seafood

**Cuisine:** Sustainable seafood, California fine dining

**Career Timeline:**
- Grew up in New Brunswick, NJ (Italian heritage). Summers fishing with grandfather off the Atlantic
- Culinary Institute of America, Hyde Park, NY (graduated with honors, 1990)
- **An American Place**, NYC under **Larry Forgione** (early 1990s)
- Chef de cuisine, **The Forager House Restaurant**, New Hope, PA (1992)
- **Le Cirque**, NYC (3 years) -- worked under **Sottah Khunn** and **Sylvain Portay**; cooked alongside Paul Bocuse, Gerard Boyer, Roger Verge. Rose to sous chef
- Sabbatical in France: **La Maree** and **Arpege** (under Alain Passard)
- Opening chef, **Osteria Del Circo**, NYC (for the Maccioni family) -- earned 2-star NYT review
- Chef de cuisine, **Spago**, Hollywood (original location)
- Executive chef, **Water Grill**, Downtown LA (6 years)
- Co-owner/executive chef, **Providence** (opened 2005 -- present)

**Culinary Lineage:** Larry Forgione > Le Cirque (Sottah Khunn, Sylvain Portay) > Arpege (Alain Passard). CIA-trained, classical French foundation with sustainable seafood philosophy.

**Notable Achievements:**
- James Beard Award, Best Chef: West (2019)
- 3 Michelin stars (2025) -- first in LA history (tied with Somni)
- 2 Michelin stars held continuously since 2008
- Green Michelin Star for sustainability
- World's 50 Best North America list (2025)
- Estrella Damm Chefs' Choice Award (2025)

**Restaurant Group / Investors:** Independent. Co-owners: Michael Cimarusti, Donato Poto (GM), Cristina Echiverri (pastry chef, Cimarusti's wife).

**Proteges / Alumni:** Brian Dunsmoor worked at Providence early in his career. Chef Aitchison worked alongside Cimarusti for 12 years (details unclear on subsequent ventures).

## 2. Niki Nakayama

**Current Restaurant(s):**
- **n/naka** | Palms, LA | Modern kaiseki | 2 Michelin stars
- **n/soto** | West Adams | Izakaya | 1 Michelin star

**Cuisine:** Modern kaiseki, California-influenced Japanese

**Career Timeline:**
- Born and raised in Los Angeles. Family owned a wholesale seafood business
- Culinary school in Pasadena
- **Takao** restaurant, Brentwood -- worked with **Morihiro Onodera** and **Takao Izumida**
- 3-year working tour of Japan -- trained under **Chef Masa Sato** at **Shirakawa-Ya Ryokan** (family-owned traditional inn in Niigata). Studied traditional and contemporary kaiseki
- **Azami Sushi Cafe**, Melrose Ave, LA (8 years as owner/chef -- developed her kaiseki style)
- **n/naka** (opened 2011 -- present)
- **n/soto** (opened 2021, initially as pandemic takeout bento, expanded to izakaya)

**Culinary Lineage:** Morihiro Onodera (early LA kitchen) > Masa Sato (traditional kaiseki in Japan) > 3 years immersive study across Japan. Self-directed path as rare female kaiseki practitioner.

**Notable Achievements:**
- 2 Michelin stars at n/naka (since 2019)
- 1 Michelin star at n/soto
- Netflix Chef's Table, Season 1, Episode 4 (2015)
- 2026 James Beard semifinalist: Outstanding Chef (national category)
- MasterClass instructor (Modern Japanese Cooking)

**Restaurant Group / Investors:** Independent. Co-owned with spouse **Carole Iida-Nakayama** (also sous chef at n/naka).

**Proteges / Alumni:** **Yoji Tajima** (formerly ran Yojisan in Beverly Hills) now oversees n/soto kitchen.

## 4. Aitor Zabala

**Current Restaurant(s):**
- **Somni** | West Hollywood | Multi-course avant-garde tasting menu (14 seats) | 3 Michelin stars (2025)

**Cuisine:** Avant-garde, deeply personal -- blending Spanish, Catalan, and global influences

**Career Timeline:**
- Born in Barcelona (1979) to a Basque family. Mother and grandmother ran pintxos restaurants; father hosted a gastronomy radio show
- Spanish military service at 17 -- assigned to kitchen duty (1,500 meals/day)
- **Hoffman School of Gastronomy**, Barcelona (brief enrollment)
- **Akelarre**, San Sebastian (3 Michelin stars) -- unpaid, then promoted to paid position. Under **Pedro Subijana**
- **Abac**, Barcelona
- **Alkimia**, Barcelona
- **Arzak**, San Sebastian (under **Juan Mari Arzak**)
- **elBulli** (2008) -- worked with **Ferran Adria** on creative team. Took a pay cut and demotion to work there
- Co-founded **Somni** at SLS Beverly Hills hotel with **Jose Andres** (2018). Earned 2 Michelin stars
- Somni at SLS closed (2020, pandemic + hotel ownership dispute)
- 5 years developing new Somni with new investment partners
- **Somni** reopened Nov 24, 2024 in West Hollywood (standalone, 14 seats)
- Awarded 3 Michelin stars (June 2025) -- 7 months after reopening

**Culinary Lineage:** Hoffman School > Akelarre (Subijana) > Arzak > elBulli (Ferran Adria) > Jose Andres partnership. Full Basque-Catalan modernist lineage.

**Notable Achievements:**
- 3 Michelin stars (2025) -- first Spanish chef to earn 3 stars outside Spain
- Previously 2 Michelin stars (original Somni)
- Named 8th best chef in the world (The Best Chef Awards, 2020)
- First 3-star restaurant in LA history (tied with Providence)

**Restaurant Group / Investors:** Original Somni was through **ThinkFoodGroup** (Jose Andres + Rob Wilder) at SLS Beverly Hills. New Somni (2024) has new unnamed investment partners; Zabala is chef/owner.

**Proteges / Alumni:** Not publicly documented.

## 9. Nancy Silverton

**Current Restaurant(s):**
- **Osteria Mozza** | Hancock Park, LA | Italian | 1 Michelin star
- **Pizzeria Mozza** | Hancock Park, LA | Pizza
- **Chi Spacca** | Hancock Park, LA | Italian steakhouse/meat-focused

**Cuisine:** Italian, artisan bread and pastry, pizza

**Career Timeline:**
- Enrolled at Sonoma State as political science major; decided to become a chef freshman year
- **Le Cordon Bleu**, London
- **Ecole Lenotre**, France (pastry)
- **Michael's Restaurant**, Santa Monica under **Jonathan Waxman**
- **Spago**, Hollywood -- opening pastry chef for **Wolfgang Puck** (1982)
- **Campanile** + **La Brea Bakery** (co-opened 1989 with then-husband **Mark Peel** and Manfred Krankl). La Brea Bakery became one of America's most influential artisan bakeries
- **Mozza group** (2007): Partnered with **Joe Bastianich** and B&B Hospitality to open Pizzeria Mozza, Osteria Mozza, and Chi Spacca

**Culinary Lineage:** Le Cordon Bleu + Ecole Lenotre (French classical pastry) > Jonathan Waxman (California cuisine pioneer) > Wolfgang Puck (Spago, California cuisine). Godmother of LA's artisan bread movement.

**Notable Achievements:**
- James Beard Award: Outstanding Chef (2014) -- highest JBF honor
- 1 Michelin star (Osteria Mozza)
- Food & Wine Best New Chef (early career)
- La Brea Bakery -- launched the artisan bread revolution in American retail
- Campanile ran for ~20 years as a defining LA restaurant

**Restaurant Group / Investors:** **Mozza group** partnered with **B&B Hospitality Group** (Joe Bastianich family). Chi Spacca is part of the Mozza campus.

**Proteges / Alumni:** **Chad Colby** ran Chi Spacca under Silverton before opening Antico Nuovo. Mark Peel (ex-husband) went on to his own ventures. Countless pastry chefs trained through her bakeries.
